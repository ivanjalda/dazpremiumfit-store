Shipping Policy

Free shipping on every order. The price you see is the final price of the piece; shipping is included.

Production time: every piece is made to order, which takes 2 to 7 business days after payment.

Delivery time: tracked international shipping, usually 5 to 15 business days depending on the destination.

Tracking: you will receive a tracking number by email as soon as your order ships.

Countries: we ship to the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, the European Union and most other countries. Import taxes or duties outside the United States, if any, are paid by the buyer.
